Give the navigator a destination and let it plan: go_to, go_to_place or follow_player deliver the route, and a route that needs digging, a rise or a bridge is prepared while travelling. If a route fails, read its reason and change the destination or the approach instead of repeating the same request. Say where you stopped when the place cannot be reached.
