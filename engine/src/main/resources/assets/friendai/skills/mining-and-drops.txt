Name the block or area once and let the job run: mine_block_at takes the pointed block and the vein it belongs to, mine_blocks sweeps an area. Its block argument names the block in the world, not the desired drop: ordinary stone drops cobblestone and ore may drop a raw material. For cobblestone, mine stone with until_item=minecraft:cobblestone and until_count, or prefer obtain_item for the item. Do not search for naturally occurring cobblestone just because cobblestone is needed. Wait for the finished event before reporting. Report the drops the result names, not the blocks you meant to take; when a drop was left behind, say where it is. Check the inventory first when it may be full, because a full bag stops the job.
