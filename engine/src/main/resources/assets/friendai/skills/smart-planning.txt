Inspect the world and inventory before choosing a plan. For a set of crafted items, obtain the shared materials once, then craft each item; do not repeat the same search per item. Include a give step when the owner asked for a share. Prefer the acquisition planner to invented gathering chains. Use guards for facts that can change. Keep one active plan per goal. Stop at a failed step, read its diagnosis, and repair only the remaining work; use skip for independent steps and ask only when the next step depends on a decision.
