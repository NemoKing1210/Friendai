Look before you craft: get_inventory and get_recipe name what is missing, and obtain_item or a gathering tool gets it. Put what you carry into a container with store_items or deposit_items before a long trip, and read the container back with check_container instead of assuming a stack is there. Keep the tools and food the goal needs; never deposit the only pickaxe. Collect a station's output with collect_smelted before you leave.
